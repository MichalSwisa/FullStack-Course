Featured Artists

Vincent van Gogh

Vincent van Gogh was a Dutch painter known for his expressive use of color, bold brushstrokes, and emotional style. Although he was not widely successful during his lifetime, he later became one of the most famous artists in history. Some of his best-known works include The Starry Night, Sunflowers, and several self-portraits.

Claude Monet

Claude Monet was a French painter and one of the founders of the Impressionist movement. He was especially interested in light, color, and the way scenes change at different times of day. Monet often painted the same subject many times under different conditions. His famous works include Water Lilies, Impression, Sunrise, and paintings of his garden in Giverny.

View Monet's artwork

Frida Kahlo

Frida Kahlo was a Mexican artist best known for her powerful self-portraits and paintings inspired by her personal experiences, identity, and Mexican culture. Her art often included symbolic images and explored themes such as pain, strength, and identity. Today, she is one of the most recognizable artists of the twentieth century.
